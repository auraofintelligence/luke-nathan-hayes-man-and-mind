# About this site

The Mind Behind the Man is not a conventional portfolio, campaign site or pitch deck. It begins with Luke Nathan Hayes as a person on Minjerribah, then follows the connections between practical work, a personal Aura, music, relationships, civic design, travel, planetary ideas and speculative futures.

Each major page carries two perspectives:

- A first-person editorial draft, synthesised from Luke's supplied material, speaks about his memory, motivations, beliefs and questions. It remains subject to Luke's direct review and revision.
- A third-person record explains what the listed sources can support, while naming gaps and uncertainty.

Every substantial project or claim is labelled as lived, built, supported by a submission document, proposed, modelled, frontier, story or an open question. The labels are not a ranking. They prevent a song, a prototype and a documented action from being mistaken for the same kind of thing. Public lodgement of supplied submission documents remains to be confirmed.

Visitors can choose a reason for being here. That choice only highlights a path through the same site. It never creates a different truth or hides the rest of the record.
