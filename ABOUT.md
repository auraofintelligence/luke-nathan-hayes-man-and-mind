# About this site

The Mind Behind the Man is not a conventional portfolio, campaign site or pitch deck. It begins with Luke Nathan Hayes as one person on Minjerribah, then follows the living connections between practical work, a personal Aura, music, relationships, civic design, travel, planetary ideas and speculative futures.

The story is told in one coherent voice. Luke Catalyst, Strange but True, i C. infinity, Tiggy Bestmann, Australian Sire and Aura of Intelligence are not presented as separate corporate brands. They are different creative and practical ways one man speaks, works, sings and imagines.

Radical transparency here means leaving the joins visible. Old documents can stay rough. Proposals say when they have not been built. Songs and fiction are allowed to carry truths that do not pretend to be engineering reports. AI-assisted work says so in its source record. Missing files remain missing instead of being quietly replaced.

The 288 outside facets of the horn torus carry every registered document, image, public project and social doorway. The camera remains outside the form. Visitors can turn it, move closer, select a facet and follow its source thread without being taken into a hidden interior.

Visitors can also choose why they arrived. That choice suggests a path through the same open site. It does not profile them, hide difficult material or create a different version of Luke.
