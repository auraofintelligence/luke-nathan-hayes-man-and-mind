12Choose your door

You do not have to follow my path in order. Start where your own curiosity catches. If we knew each other years ago, begin with the bloke and the break. If you build strange things, go straight under the Aura.

Your chosen door is a recommendation, not a box. You can change it, ignore it or open every page.
