The Mind Behind the Man

I’m Luke. I’ve moved house more than 40 times, worked all sorts of jobs and followed my curiosity overseas. These days I’m on Minjerribah, making music, building things and still asking far too many questions.

As a kid I loved hide-and-seek and tiggy. They were explorer games. I even named my first dog Tiggy. That urge to explore has stayed with me through new towns, countries, relationships and ideas.

I’ve been a mechanic, a festival worker, a driver and a web designer, among plenty of other things. I love a good conversation, live music and the ocean. Somewhere along the way, my questions about how we live became years of work on AI and the future. This site brings that work back together with the life it came from.
