First-person draft

Drafted in my voice

I rejected the status quo in 2012. I did not replace it with one grand answer. I started building a personal Aura: songs, software, civic tools, strange questions, relationship worlds, island plans and routes to the stars.

This is my mirror ball. Every facet is mine. None of it is your instruction. Take the reflection that helps, question the rest, and build your own. I am showing the links between my life and my ideas because hiding the joins would make both less honest.

Third person

From the record

This portrait is assembled from Luke's own photographs, historical documents, documents supplied as public submissions, working websites, music and first-person accounts. It is a contextual reading of a self-authored archive, not an independent biography and not a claim that every idea has been realised.

The record shows a recurring movement from a personal question to a public experiment. It also contains gaps, changing versions and AI-assisted design material. Those limits stay visible through the evidence labels and the source room.
