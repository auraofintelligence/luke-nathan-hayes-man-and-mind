First-person draft

Drafted in my voice

I live and work on Minjerribah. Strange but True starts with small useful things: a market table, a technology problem, a recording job, a community event or a yarn that helps somebody move forward.

The same island also makes me ask very large questions. Could shared compute, training and creative facilities grow from Dunwich? Could digital twins help us rehearse choices? What could careful, properly authorised study of sand and minerals teach us about resilience and future materials? Those are proposals and models. They are not promises made on behalf of Quandamooka people or the wider community.

Third person

From the record

This page draws on the current Strange but True artefacts, documents described as Luke's AI and data-centre submission and Fair Go companion, and the live ready SET Co-op, Straddie Twins, Grain by Grain and Extreme Matter Atlas projects. These are Luke-authored or Luke-directed records, not evidence of local cultural authority or public approval.

The record supports Luke's residence and practical work on the island, public prototypes, documents supplied as submissions, music and story assets. It does not establish a funded Dunwich node, an operating ready SET Co-op or fabricated custom Aura hardware.
