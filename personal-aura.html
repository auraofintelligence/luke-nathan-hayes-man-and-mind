My Aura has 288 facets

Aura is Luke's attempt to give one complicated human life a shape without flattening it. The horn torus holds 288 outside facets: many ways in, one moving person, and no demand that anybody else use the same map.

Enter this chapter Open the studio archive

Aura is not a standard profile that tells everyone what a human should be. It is my attempt to make my own complexity understandable, navigable and extendable. Body, mind, memory, place, work, love, family, music, humour, law, civics, science, travel, Country, Earth, space, mystery and future all affect one another.

I make parts of it public because another person may recognise a useful reflection. That does not give me authority over their design. Sovereignty is not the same as secrecy. A person might choose privacy, radical disclosure, selective sharing, community custody, forgetting, inheritance or something else. What matters to me is authorship, context and informed choice.

The facets are related

A facet is a doorway into the same life, not a separate score or a permanent label.

Body, mind and memory

The personal Aura starts with lived experience and the attempt to connect physical state, reflection, memory and choice. Current diagrams model relationships; they do not read a whole person automatically.

Work, love and place

Practical work, relationships and Minjerribah are not side notes to the architecture. They are among the conditions that give Luke's projects meaning and keep the system personal.

Public reflections

The live Aura gateway generalises selected ideas into projects others can inspect, question or remix. Publication makes them accessible, not universal or compulsory.

Follow this into the live work

Self-authorship needs choices

A self-sovereign system should support different boundaries without quietly ranking one life pattern above another.

Permission begins with the person

The Alpha Infinity artefact joins conscience, learning, love and self-permission. It records Luke's values and visual language at that time, not a rule for other people.

Disclosure is not a virtue test

Luke's idea of personal constitutional agency allows privacy, openness and mixed forms. The relevant questions are who chooses, what they understand and whether that choice can change.

The model stays unfinished

Aura has changed across versions and will change again. The public record should preserve those versions instead of pretending the newest diagram was always the plan.
