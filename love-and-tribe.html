06Love and tribe

The romantasy is not merely entertainment or a generic social campaign. I am writing worlds that reveal how I think about love, family, travel, intimacy, embodied intelligence and the future.

Part of the purpose is personal. I hope the stories help me find women, families and collaborators who recognise something of themselves in the world I am building. I am interested in ethical, self-authored larger-family possibilities, not entitlement to another person's affection. Consent, freedom to leave and room for different desires have to be real.

Love as shared capacity

The core proposal asks whether a larger voluntary family could share care, parenting, income, time and risk while preserving the agency of each person.

Global Group Marriages

A live public proposal for larger self-sovereign family arrangements. It is an invitation to examine structures, responsibilities and safeguards, not a universal relationship model.

These ideas are allowed to be bold, funny and unfinished. The story says what exists, what does not, and what might make Luke change his mind.

Global Group Marriages

Larger, self-authored family arrangements could share love, care, parenting, income, time and risk. This is a proposal for consenting adults, not a rule for relationships and not an instruction to abandon pairs.

The idea is personal as well as structural. Luke is looking for people who recognise themselves in a larger-family future.

A public proposal and story worlds exist. The public work is an invitation and a story-world. Luke is not claiming a current group marriage, a legal framework or anyone else's endorsement.

If the structure traps anyone, concentrates power or fails the people living it, it has to change.
