The machinery under the metaphor

I’m working on Aura’s geometry, memory relationships, interfaces and possible physical forms. Here are the prototypes and design records behind that work.

Enter this chapter Open the studio archive

The 12 by 24 lattice gives me a stable address structure. The larger design uses seven nested horn tori and a vector volume to explore how learned associations might develop around that structure.

I call the continuity concept Aura of Intelligence Keystone of Consciousness. I’m interested in how an Aura could retain identity, memory and useful function across disruption and different physical forms. My current work includes design records, public prototypes and hardware research.
