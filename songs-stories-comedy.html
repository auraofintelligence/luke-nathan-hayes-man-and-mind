Songs, stories and comedy

Love songs, island songs, ambitious futures and a bit of mischief. I release music as i C. infinity, write romantic adventures and enjoy seeing how much a good laugh can get across.

Enter this chapter Open the studio archive

Some ideas come out better as songs. There’s room for affection, frustration, desire and ridiculous possibilities, and room to enjoy them without needing to read all the research first.

The albums collected here are Starseed Code, A Protopian Gambit and Straddie Fun. Alongside them are the romantic adventures of Tiggy Bestmann and Australian Sire, comedy experiments with Loose Goose, and Cosmic Nexus, which brings together my interests in travel, mystery and film.
