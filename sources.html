Show me the receipts

First-person draft

Drafted in my voice

I am not asking anybody to accept a leap because I say it confidently. I want the document, image, song, submission, website or design conversation sitting close enough to inspect.

Some sources are rough, duplicated or AI-assisted. I would rather preserve their history and label it than quietly tidy the archive into a story that never happened.

Third person

From the record

The register catalogues supplied filenames and public URLs across personal, historical, creative, technical and civic material. Most were authored, directed or selected by Luke. The room provides provenance, not independent verification of every claim inside a source.

Project Atlas can help establish public project dates and connections. GitHub's latest modified date is not treated as a project's origin date, and missing originals remain marked as missing until they are found.

Evidence lens. All states are visible until you choose otherwise.

What each record should reveal

Source cards keep the original context instead of reducing everything to a download button.

Built

Originals and versions

Preserve supplied filenames and group related versions. Do not delete an older file merely because a newer one looks cleaner.

Open question

Authorship and AI involvement

Distinguish Luke original, Luke-directed AI collaboration, preserved AI response, document supplied as a submission, personal image, generated concept artwork and genuinely external source.

Built

Public links and checked dates

Record whether live URLs resolve without copying whole external sites. Use Project Atlas and original records for origin dates, while noting any unresolved provenance.
