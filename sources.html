Chapter 11 of 13 | Sources

Show me the receipts

This is the open studio floor: original files, public links, rough drafts, AI conversations, songs, dates, missing pieces and changing versions left close enough to inspect.

Enter this chapter Open the studio archive
11Sources

I am not asking anybody to accept a leap because I say it confidently. I want the document, image, song, submission, website or design conversation sitting close enough to inspect.

Some sources are rough, duplicated or AI-assisted. I would rather preserve their history and describe it honestly than quietly tidy the archive into a story that never happened.

Leave the fingerprints on it

A source is more interesting when you can still see how it arrived, who touched it and what changed on the way.

Keep the earlier drafts

The old filename, the rough version and the cleaner revision belong beside one another. Tidiness is not worth quietly rewriting the path that got us here.

Say who made what

Some pieces are mine, some were made with AI under my direction, some preserve another AI's response, and some came from elsewhere. I would rather name those hands than perform a false lone-genius act.

Let the links lead somewhere

A public thread should open where it says it will. Project Atlas and the original records hold the dates; where the beginning is still fuzzy, the fuzziness stays visible.

Follow this into the live work

Do not make the image do all the talking

A text-heavy image deserves words people can actually read. A dramatic crop can live in the story while the complete original waits here in the open studio.
