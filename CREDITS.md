## Horn torus

The interactive 12 by 24 horn torus is adapted from Luke's original [Aura Horn Torus](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-horn-torus/) geometry. The portfolio version renders all 288 outside facets and keeps the camera outside the surface. Its 192 broad outer facets carry the site's source map; the four cramped rows curling into the horn stay quiet. It uses Canvas and ordinary JavaScript with no vector image files or icon libraries.

Based on aura-horn-torus by Luke Nathan Hayes / Strange But True / Aura of Intelligence.
