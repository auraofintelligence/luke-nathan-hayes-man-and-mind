The bloke before the blueprint

I live at Amity on Minjerribah. My work has included construction, warehouses, driving, airport services, festivals, mechanics and customer service. I started web design in 2008/09, then went through NEIS with Sarina Russo and kept building independent media, research and AI projects. I also write songs and books.

I have worked in construction, warehouses, market research, mail and truck driving, mine shutdowns, airport services, rail, festivals, mechanics, retail and customer service. I started web design in 2008/09, shortly before doing NEIS with Sarina Russo. From there I built websites and developed independent research, media, community and AI projects.

I use different names for different work. Luke Catalyst is for public systems and AI work. Strange but True is my sole-trader business. i C. infinity is my music. Tiggy Bestmann and Australian Sire are names used in my fiction. Aura of Intelligence is my long-term cognitive architecture project.

My work history

I have moved between physical work, service work, technology, community media and independent projects.

Web design started in 2008/09

I began doing web design in 2008/09, shortly before starting NEIS with Sarina Russo. That early work included CreatingAttraction.com.au and led into later independent websites and projects.

Practical work from 2000 to 2007

I worked in customer service and perishables at Woolworths, automotive repair at Drive Safe Service, events in London and Brisbane, Queensland Rail, airport services in Darwin and mine shutdowns with Ausblast. I also completed automotive training, gained a heavy-rigid truck licence and received airside security training.

Business and learning from 2008 to 2012

I started web design in 2008/09 and established CreatingAttraction.com.au through NEIS with Sarina Russo. During this period I also worked for Australia Post, Kirby Vacuum Cleaners and Viande Fine Foods, studied neuro-linguistic programming and entrepreneurship, researched Australian and international law, and hosted a podcast series that reached more than 80,000 listens.

Island, media and systems work from 2013 to 2017

The timeline records owner-builder work at Amity Point, I Choose Infinity, web administration for Maringa Farm, aural-history transcription for the North Stradbroke Island Historical Museum, Island Vibe and Quandamooka Festival volunteering, GamifyDemocracy.com, Quandamooka Festival web design, StraddieInfo.com and AuraOZ. It also records game-design study and machine-learning and AI education.

Work that exists now

I currently take on construction and forklift work while building Strange but True. The business offers practical local help with technology, AI, media, events and projects.

Things still being proposed

The Dunwich cultural intelligence node and ready SET Co-op do not currently exist. They are proposals for shared local facilities, training and work.

A bloke who keeps the joke

I use humour in my writing, music and public work. The comedy material is part of the creative record, not evidence for the technical claims elsewhere on this site.
