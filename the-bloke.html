The bloke before the blueprint

Before the tori, satellites and civic machinery, there is Luke: living at Amity on Minjerribah, doing practical work, making songs, having a laugh and trying to connect large questions to useful local action.

First-person draft

Drafted in my voice

I am not a clean job title. I have worked with my hands, travelled, sold things at markets, helped people with technology, recorded community events, written songs and kept following questions that refused to stay in one box.

Luke Nathan Hayes is the person. Luke Catalyst is the public systems and ideas identity. Strange but True is the work I can actually offer now. i C. infinity carries the music. Aura of Intelligence holds the long arc. Those names are different doors into one life, not a collection of companies pretending to be bigger than they are.

Third person

From the record

This account is grounded in Luke's 2024 visual CV, Strange but True business card, current sole-trader site and earlier Luke Catalyst site. These are mostly self-authored sources, so they establish what Luke has recorded and presented, not an outside endorsement of his abilities.

The available record supports a practical history across local service, technology, media, events, travel and project work. It is not a complete employment history, and the personal archive still has missing periods and photographs.
