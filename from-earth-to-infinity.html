From one Aura to a solar system

My projects range from personal Aura to Oceania, Earth and the solar system. I’m interested in how intelligence, relationships and ways of living might develop across those scales.

Enter this chapter Open the studio archive

I keep extending the questions from one personal Aura to families, communities, islands, Earth and space. GAJRA Earth, the Solar Swarm and the Cities of Light explore different parts of that larger picture.

Joyful Responsible Abundance is the phrase I use for the future I want to explore. The projects give me ways to work through it, from practical tools and research to music and imagined worlds.
